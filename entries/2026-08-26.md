We thought we'd take it easy today and just chill out at the hotel. The news of Dolly Parton's passing made me weepy as I watched all the video clips people were posting. I'd never heard her cover of [Stairway to Heaven](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fw_Codf29Pw) until today. Jolene has always been one of my all-time favorites. You know how you can tell Ticketmaster to let you know when certain artists are coming to your city — well, for female artists, I only have two on that list: Tracy Chapman and Dolly Parton. She's the Queen of Everything, really.

Most of our meals have been wonderful, and today's lunch was no exception. Morning glory fried with garlic is Thomas's favorite vegetable dish, so we always order it. We had fried fish — can't remember the name — and pumpkin soup with pork ribs. The soup was humongous, but we ate most of it anyway because it was so good and I didn't want to waste any of it.

![](../images/2026-08-26/IMG_2267.jpeg)

It makes complete sense that Vietnamese people are at the beach at 5:00 AM and in the evenings, when the temperature isn't so brutal. There's a Zumba class that starts at 5:15 AM every morning, and oh, we could hear it. This evening we walked down to the beach to be among the locals. Playing or exercising, they were at it again. Two women covered their legs and arms in mud from the sand. I've heard mud baths are good for you, but it looked like a lot of work to rinse off afterward.

Tonight Vietnam's soccer team played Thailand again for the ASEAN final. Vietnam would win the title even with a draw, since they'd already beaten Thailand 2-0 a few days earlier. We went around the corner to Sala Cafe, owned by the same hotel we're staying at, to sit outside and watch the game with everyone else. Wow, what a game — Thailand scored first, then scored again to go up 2-0, and Vietnam didn't tie it up until the final few minutes. Seeing that we were out of beer, a young man came over to our table and poured us a new can so we could cheer with them. I know that gesture meant a lot to Thomas, since he loves the people here and wishes he could communicate with them.

Even in a small city, the streets filled with motorbikes honking and cheering for hours. The game ended at 10 PM here, and the celebration lasted well past midnight.
