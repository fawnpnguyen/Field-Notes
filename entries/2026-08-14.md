title: Asking for a Map of a Place with One Path
---
We checked out of [Iconsphere Hotel](https://iconsphere.hotelshochiminhcity.net/en/) and got a private car ride — 3 hours — to Cần Thơ in the Mekong Delta. We're staying at [Green Village Mekong](https://www.greenvillagemekong.com/) with just 8 bamboo bungalows. Each is spacious with 2 large beds. Over each bed is a mosquito net contraption that tells me I still better lather up with mosquito repellent tonight. Quite a contrast from the hustle and bustle of Sài Gòn.

The young man (call me "Key," he says) greeting us is barefoot and is surprised that I can speak clear-enough Vietnamese for having left eons (50 years) ago. While waiting for our room to be cleaned and ready, we ordered lunch from a limited menu. We both ordered the beef stew, mine with broth and noodles, Thomas with crispy noodles. My noodles turned out to be the kind you get in a ramen package. I don't even recognize the noodles on Thomas's plate. He ate everything, though, and helped himself to some of the broth.

Our accommodation comes with dinner served from 6 to 8. I'm less enthusiastic about it after getting packaged ramen noodles for lunch. It is a charming spot. I asked for a map of the place and both kids (Key and Chloe) laughed. They said it's small and just has a circular path with the pool at one end. I felt silly after walking the path.

We took a nap and woke up in time for dinner. I showered first and immediately missed the shower head from the last hotel. I had to shower so I could rub all exposed body parts with bug repellent lotion, including my face. There was a family of five and three other couples. I'm the only Asian guest here. We were served chicken cooked in fish sauce, so it's braised slices of chicken, like chicken "kho," and I like it. We also had chicken hotpot brought out along with a plate of vegetables: 3 kinds of mushrooms, cabbage, and morning glory. The broth had already been simmered with chicken pieces, large carrot rounds, and equally sized corn rounds. Key places half of the veggies into the pot and tells us to wait 3 to 4 minutes. It was delicious! I'm trying to eat all the veggies and fruits while here. (Yeah, I ate over half a kilo of longans this morning in one sitting. Offered none of it to Thomas because I already knew he'd say no. They were the biggest longans, almost the size of golf balls!)

We're here for 3 nights. Key suggested two tours during our stay: [Cái Răng Floating Market](https://www.greenvillagemekong.com/floating-market-tour-by-boat) or [pagoda/bamboo village/mangrove forest](https://www.greenvillagemekong.com/mangrove-forest-lung-ngoc-hoang). Both tours are 5 hours each, as 2 of those are for getting there and back. We said we'd think about it and let him know in the morning. But I knew I needed a body massage (90-minute massage for 350,000 VND) and booked one for 10:00 AM tomorrow. There's only one lady who does this and she just comes to our room.
