title: Asking for a Map of a Place with One Path
---

We checked out of [IconSphere Hotel](https://iconsphere.hotelshochiminhcity.net/en/) and got a private car ride — 3 hours — to Cần Thơ in the Mekong Delta. We're staying at [Green Village Mekong](https://www.greenvillagemekong.com/) with just 8 bamboo bungalows. Each is spacious with 2 large beds. Over each bed is a mosquito net contraption that tells me I still better lather up with mosquito repellent tonight. Quite a contrast from the hustle and bustle of Sài Gòn.

The young man (call me "Key," he says) greeting us is barefoot and is surprised that I can speak clear-enough Vietnamese for having left eons (50 years) ago. While waiting for our room to be cleaned and ready, we ordered lunch from a limited menu. We both ordered the beef stew, mine with broth and noodles, Thomas with crispy noodles. My noodles turned out to be the kind you get in a ramen package. I don't even recognize the noodles on Thomas's plate. He ate everything though and helped himself to some of the broth.

Our accommodation comes with dinner served from 6 to 8. I'm less enthusiastic about it after getting packaged ramen noodles for lunch. It is a charming spot. I asked for a map of the place and both kids (Key and Chloe) laughed. They said it's small and just has a circular path with the pool at one end. I felt silly after walking the path.

We're here for 3 nights. Key suggested we could do the floating market or boat ride in the morning. I'm thinking of the 90-minute massage for 350,000 VND.
