title: Everyone Is Asking You and Nobody Cares to Ask Me
---

We went to [Chợ Bến Thành](https://benthanhmarket.vn/) this morning, a 15-minute walk from our hotel. Thomas picked up a pair of prescription sunglasses from a shop just a block from the market. He's gotten a new pair each of the last four years we've been in Vietnam, because they're just over $100 instead of the $300+ he was quoted in the States — and that's with insurance. This market is really exclusively for tourists now; it's overpriced and extra crowded. Every vendor asks Thomas what he wants to buy and ignores me. I finally said out loud to Thomas, "Everyone is asking you and nobody cares to ask me." A shop owner sitting right there probably heard me and asked if I wanted to buy something. I smiled and said cảm ơn, confirming to her that it was a waste of breath to ask me anyway.

![](../images/2026-08-30/IMG_2309.jpeg)

We went back to this coffee shop that had real coconut coffee. I noticed there's a bánh mì place in the same building, Grandma Lu's, on the 5th floor. Turns out it was one of the best I've had — I ordered the [truffle mushroom and egg](https://banhmigrandmalu.com/products/banh-mi-trung-nam-trufle), and it was perfect. I actually loved that it was smaller, about two-thirds of the normal size I see. I took these pictures while waiting for the sandwich.
