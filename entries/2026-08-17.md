title: Miền Quê Nostalgia in Cần Thơ
date: 2026-08-17
---

Growing up, I loved going to see my Dad's nephew, who has a wife and a young kid. The reason is they live in "miền quê" — Vietnamese for farmland, countryside. This hotel in Cần Thơ reminds me of that. It's green and peaceful. I bet everyone knows everyone here. We walked around the neighborhood and saw some beautiful homes with marble driveways and iron gates. Some even have family burial plots, massive marble ones, not just a sign of respect but also of wealth, I think.

I do miss access to all the foods, though. A big part of why I come to Vietnam is for the food, and not having that makes the three-night stay just enough. There are two dogs and a cat in residence, and I almost envy their stress-free life. Happy that they, and all animals, get to live in this slice of heaven. When I mentioned this, Chloe assured me that Coffee, the dog, works hard at night watching over this place.

![](../images/2026-08-17/IMG_4290.jpeg)

It took over 4 hours for us to get back to Sài Gòn, traffic for sure, but also our driver decided to take another route?! We're here for just one night at one of our favorite hotels, [La Siesta Premium Saigon Central](https://lasiestahotels.com/saigoncentral/). We've stayed in 3 of the 6 they have in Vietnam. Simple elegance, great service. We're a 5-minute walk from [Ben Thanh Market](https://benthanhmarket.com.vn/). It's amazing how one food stall manages to carry 90% of the dishes you'd want. And what they don't have, they'll just order it for you and bring it to your seat.

I treated myself to a 45-minute shampoo/massage for 375,000 VND. My scalp and hair never had it so good. Like wow. Why can't I have this every other day.
