title: iCloud Tried to Kill the Talk. The Talk Won.
---

I just got home from Pittsburgh. I was invited to be the keynote speaker for their 75th anniversary math conference. Really a great honor, so I built a brand new talk, "Stop Making Math Useful." I spent months on it, only to lose all of it due to fucking iCloud. All my keynote slides vanished. Seven hours and three Apple agents later, nothing was retrieved. Amplify IT couldn't help either. So I cried and rebuilt from scratch. Luckily I had a week to do so. Can't imagine if this had happened a day or two before.

I really wanted to tell this story, this message about math teaching. It resonated with the folks. They were very kind and receptive. People kept telling me how much they enjoyed it. One lady in the elevator kept saying that my talk was "phenomenal." That's a big deal! Made all the hard work and hours poured in worth it.

I now have the timing down and will tweak a few things. Yay me!

I'm always tired, but the last 10 days were peak. The stress was unreal. My eyeballs have been hurting, like they ran a marathon. My eye sockets hurt. Watery. Retirement needs to happen or I'll die from stress.
