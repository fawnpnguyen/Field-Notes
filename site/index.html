We leave for Vietnam this evening. Our 4th year in a row, made possible by Amplify. I've never had a job that allowed this kind of time off and the funds to do so. Wish we could have done this as a family, meaning taking all 3 kids at one point when they were younger. That still remains a dream. Having all 3 kids together in one place, celebrating each one of them for existing and making the best of what they have and where they are.

I learned yesterday that Nicolai quit his job 3 weeks ago. I'm actually surprised he hadn't done it sooner. He's weathered through all his bosses, stayed longer than anyone else. Well, yeah, that was his one and only job since graduating from UCSB. He actually started with them the summer before, between his junior and senior year. So proud of him for sticking with it. He's spending this time just exploring, much needed I'm sure.

Sabrina has made it back to Scotland. Her VISA expired for South Korea. She did say she'll try to make it back to Seoul someday.

Gabriel got offered a job on the spot yesterday! Up in Summerland, so it's a bit of a drive, 30 miles, not bad, especially since he'll have to leave early anyway and avoid the potential traffic. He'll be a line cook, something he has a lot of experience. So happy for him. Thank you, God! He got a Juiced bike also, best purchase. He's been taking it out every day. Clears that mind and gets that body moving. If I loved and worried about that boy any more, my heart would crack open.

Thomas and I got into a big fight this morning. So dumb. I will not be talked to that way, will not tolerate his yelling. He will tell you the same. Each and every fight, I wish there's a camera on us the whole time as I of course sees that he has turned it into something it wasn't. Simply untrue. Fucking can't stand that suddenly I became the bad guy. I hate the whole "you always" bullshit. What I always do is work work work and clean clean clean. And fucking pay all the bills. Yeah, I resent this. Don't want to, but when I work my ass off only to be talked to like this makes it really easy for me to say fuck you. Go away, shut up, leave me alone. He says the lowest-IQ shit like, "You don't want peace!" Motherfucker.
